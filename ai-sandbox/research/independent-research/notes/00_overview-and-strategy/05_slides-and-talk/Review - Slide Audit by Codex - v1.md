# Review: Slide Audit by Codex (v1)

**Scope:** Independent two-pass review of the 14-slide main deck for a 30-minute PhD peer talk. Pass 1 used only the rendered deck text and PDF, excluding speaker notes and appendix. Pass 2 used the deck notes/appendix, the four specified companion files, and the three allowed transcript spot-checks. No deck edits made.

## Verdict

**Mostly.** The sequence has a sound arc—from a concrete choice, through discretion and evidence value, into three research questions and a candidate transport method. A newcomer can follow the broad purpose, but several key links rely on unexplained research shorthand, and the transport cycle/output table and closing timeline do not yet explain enough to make the proposed test and gates feel fully earned. The strongest risks are slide 3's decision tree, slide 11's unnumbered but complex feedback loop, and slide 14's gate/timeline rationale.

Tags: **[C]** checked against named file/transcript; **[I]** reviewer interpretation; **[P]** suggested wording or remedy. Ratings: **BLOCKER**, **SHOULD FIX**, **POLISH**. Deck line references below refer to `nexus_evidence_agenda_slides.marp.md`; PDF page numbers are physical slide numbers.
