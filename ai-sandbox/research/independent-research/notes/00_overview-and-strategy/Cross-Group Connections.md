## Connections to the other groups

### To the compound shocks panel (group B)
- Project 02 is "Fused Subnational Multi-Shock Studies". Phase A is the Somalia case. The 3R plan still reads Phase A as "separating the three shocks on district displacement and food prices", with DML and spatial network models as methods.
- The Pilot Scope Realignment (Oct 31) has since put formal modeling on hold behind the review gate. The 3R plan's Project 02 methods paragraph describes what Phase B may eventually do, not what Phase A is doing.
- The Data Audit found that only conflict and rainfall are dense in both countries. That is a tension with the flagship question, which needs displacement and cash-transfer presence. Cash-transfer program locations (named in the original Project 02 and the 3R plan) are not in either panel yet.
- Project 04 (toolkit) is in practice what became `reprokit` and `compound-shocks-panel`. The Pipeline Review is the first assessment of whether that reusable layer works.

### To the literature pipeline and evidence map (group C)
- Project 01 is the evidence map. The original plan names 3ie, J-PAL and WFP registries; the 3R plan splits it into disaster vs conflict bins. The Pitch's taxonomy schema (hazard types, intervention type, study type) is the machinery for that split.
- The CTL partnership in the 3R plan is the stated reason the Pitch is framed as a teachable tool.

### To RCT transportability (group D)
- Direction III in the original plan (scaling pilots with DML, causal forests, hierarchical meta-analysis, Bayesian priors, modeled on CEGA's IDEAL) is the closest ancestor of the RCT brainstorm. The brainstorm turns it into a concrete four-step design.
- The 3R plan's Project 03 uses "transferability" in a different sense: do disaster-response mechanisms (parametric triggers, forecast-based financing) work in conflict settings? That is a mechanism and policy question answered by desk review, not statistical transportability. The two should be kept distinct when writing, even if the same literature overlaps (inference).
- The original plan flags that Direction III has no named SIPA faculty anchor. The 3R plan assigns Björkegren (ML under distribution shift), which is a closer fit to the transportability work.

### To exploratory methods (group E)
- The original plan's "strand that doesn't fit" (post-Keynesian agent-based models, feminist critique, DEA and non-parametric optimization, spectral bias) is drawn from `Research Ideas.docx`. It recommends treating that strand as secondary and raising it with the advisor in the first semester.
- The 3R plan lists `Research Ideas.md` among its inputs but does not return to the question. Project 05 (a systems-dynamics scoping note on tipping points and hysteresis) is the nearest slot for the agent-based and tipping-point ideas.
