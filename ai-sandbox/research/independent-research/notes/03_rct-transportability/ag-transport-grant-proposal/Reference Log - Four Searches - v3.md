# Exploratory data analysis and brief literature check: environmental covariates and yield-response transport (v3)

*2026-11-16. Supersedes [v2](Reference%20Log%20-%20Four%20Searches%20-%20v2.md) for all numbers; [v1](Reference%20Log%20-%20Four%20Searches%20-%20v1.md) still holds the full literature tables. Very exploratory: public data, first-pass definitions, no model selection, no pre-registration. Code, tables and checksums are in [`projects/ag-transport-pilot/`](../../projects/ag-transport-pilot/README.md) and reproduce from the raw files with the scripts there.*

## 1. What we found, in six lines
1. **Coverage.** Somalia is the only country with at least 100 exposed and 100 non-exposed yield unit-years for both sorghum and maize in HarvestStat; Nigeria passes for maize only. South Sudan has no usable sorghum or maize yields after 2012. This makes Somalia the natural country-level holdout and Sudan and South Sudan the data gap.
2. **OFRA has almost no conflict-exposed trials.** Of 517 trial site-years from 2010 on, 17 have 50 or more violent events within 50 km (15 of them in Rwanda, probably border spillover from DRC and Burundi) and 17 have 10 or more events within 25 km. None are in Somalia, Sudan or South Sudan.
3. **Rainfall data exists; a naive rainfall model does not transfer.** CHIRPS seasonal rainfall matches 96% of 12,899 unit-crop-years. Within-unit rainfall explains 0.13 to 0.19 of Somali yield variation (Sudan sorghum 0.15). A slope fitted on other countries gives out-of-country R2 of about 0.02.
4. **The proxy candidates look different from Somalia on conflict exposure and similar on rainfall regime.** Somalia's nearest rainfall-regime neighbours are mostly in Niger, Kenya, Chad, Angola, Ethiopia and Senegal, only 3% conflict-exposed; Sudan's are in Burkina Faso, Niger and Chad (11% exposed). They are only slightly farther away in climate space than Somalia's own units (0.19 vs 0.14).
5. **OFRA trials sit on better land than the surrounding area.** Where a trial and a HarvestStat yield share a unit, crop and year (94 unit-years), the OFRA coefficient A is a median 1.9 times (maize) and 1.7 times (sorghum) the unit yield. This assumes A is the no-nutrient yield, which I could not verify.
6. **CIMMYT's Sudan assessment is a within-state calibration, not a transfer test.** Its NDVI-to-yield curve (R2 0.80) is an in-sample fit extended to all sorghum pixels in four states; the text I read describes no held-out validation. The funder (USAID) has since been dismantled; the programme's status is unconfirmed.

## 3. Data and definitions (details in the pilot README)
HarvestStat v1.2 yields 2010-2025 (qc flag 0); violent ACLED events 2008-2025 assigned to units; a unit-crop-year is **exposed** at 50 or more events in the calendar years spanning the crop season (200 and a density measure also written); OFRA trial exposure = events within 25 or 50 km in the trial year or the year before; CHIRPS v2.0 monthly rainfall, 2010-2023, summed over the **union of all seasons' planting-to-harvest months** for each unit-year.

## 4. Results
**4.1 Coverage (exposed / non-exposed unit-years).** Unchanged from v2: sorghum Somalia 190 / 322, Nigeria 73 / 210, Sudan 65 / 188, Burkina Faso 42 / 572, Mali 19 / 70, Ethiopia 9 / 547, South Sudan 0 / 2; maize Somalia 226 / 337, Nigeria 106 / 370, Burkina Faso 40 / 570, Kenya 20 / 684. Table: `outputs/coverage_*.csv`.

**4.2 Geographic specificity (median unit area).** Kenya counties 3,551 km2 (60 km across); Somalia districts 5,713 (76); Ethiopia zones 9,102 (95); Nigeria states 31,738 (178); Sudan states 57,263 (239); South Sudan states about 62,000 (249); Mali 81,389 (285). Somalia-to-Sudan prediction mixes scales by about a factor of ten. `outputs/unit_sizes_sorghum.csv`.

**4.3 Variability and missingness.** Within-unit log-yield SD is 0.73-0.87 in target countries vs about 0.35-0.37 elsewhere. High-exposure units report as many years as low-exposure ones in most countries (Somalia 0.78 vs 0.86; Sudan 0.96 vs 0.92). Coverage check only.

**4.4 OFRA exposure (corrected).** Country now comes from the HarvestStat polygon, because OFRA's three-letter codes are ambiguous (Nig = Nigeria or Niger; Mal = Malawi or Mali; Bur = Burundi or Burkina Faso). With ACLED added for the Sahel and DRC: 517 site-years; 353 have any event within 50 km; 104 have 10 or more; 17 have 50 or more (15 Rwanda, 2 Kenya); 17 have 10 or more within 25 km (Uganda 6, Zambia 4, Ethiopia 2, Mali 2, Rwanda 2, Nigeria 1). Niger 45 site-years, none with 10 or more events within 50 km. `outputs/ofra_acled_exposure_by_country.csv`.

**4.5 OFRA vs the surrounding unit (exploratory).** OFRA rows are trial-level points; all fall inside a HarvestStat unit. 2,922 are maize, sorghum or millet; 954 rows (113 unit-years, 2010+) share a unit, crop and year with a HarvestStat yield. For the 94 unit-years with matched maize or sorghum coefficients: median ratio of OFRA coefficient A to unit yield 1.93 (maize, 60 unit-years, interquartile 1.40-2.93) and 1.65 (sorghum, 34, 1.03-2.94); rank correlation with unit yield 0.32 and 0.41; regressing log A on log unit yield and log season rain: 0.50 and 0.47, R2 0.23. **Caveats:** the response-function equation is not read (A may not be a control yield); n is small and drawn from 48 units; the rain coefficient moved from 0.26 to 0.47 when I fixed the rainfall window, so it is not stable. What it does suggest is the shape of a useful model: unit-level yield and rainfall carry information about trial-level coefficients, so area statistics could help calibrate site-specific response before an expensive trial.

**4.6 CHIRPS (corrected; supersedes v2 and the idea summary v1).** An earlier run used one arbitrary season's months for units with two seasons; the union-of-seasons window gives: seasonal rainfall available for 12,368 of 12,899 unit-crop-years; within-unit R2 Somalia sorghum 0.19, maize 0.13, Sudan sorghum 0.15; slope fitted on non-target countries applied to Somalia or Sudan gives out-of-sample R2 of 0.02 (sorghum, Somalia and Sudan; maize, Somalia). Rainfall-regime neighbours (3 per target unit): Somalia mostly Niger, Kenya, Chad, Angola, Ethiopia, Senegal; Sudan mostly Burkina Faso, Niger, Chad; 3% and 11% conflict-exposed; median distance 0.19 vs 0.14 within Somalia. **Revised reading:** the earlier claim that proxies are "three times farther in climate space" does not hold; climate alone does not rule them out, and rainfall alone gives no transfer skill. `outputs/chirps_*.csv`, `rain_regime_*.csv`.

## 6. Limits
Exposure is a raw event count; units differ in size; HarvestStat has no gap filling and Ethiopia 2021-22 partly carries values forward; HarvestStat countries without an ACLED pull are excluded from exposure tables; the CHIRPS model is one covariate with unit anomalies; windows differ in length across years; OFRA coefficients not yet interpreted; no analysis here tests treatment-effect transfer to the target countries.
