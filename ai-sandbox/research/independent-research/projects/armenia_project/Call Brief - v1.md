# Armenia evidence map: call brief (v1)

*Prepared 2026-11-24 for the call on 2026-11-25. Keep this to two pages. Detail, reasoning and sources to verify are in [Project Notes - v1](Project%20Notes%20-%20v1.md). Client and fee details are deliberately not written here: this repository is public, so do not commit or push this file without reviewing it.*

## Purpose of the call

Learn what decisions the map must inform and what she already has. Leave with an agreed scope for a first phase, and a yes to a short written proposal. Do not agree a price on the call.

## Topline messages

1. **What she gets.** A structured picture of what has been tried in Armenia in education and health, what it achieved, and where the evidence is thin. It comes as an evidence map and an evidence gap map, plus a short memo on what looks promising.
2. **How it is different.** Every study is coded to the same intervention and outcome categories (3ie-style), so the map can be filtered and re-cut. Every claim traces to a source, and the search is logged so it can be repeated or extended.
3. **The gap map has two layers.** One layer shows evidence from Armenia. The other shows evidence from comparable countries. An intervention that is well evidenced elsewhere and absent in Armenia is her "promising but untested here" list.
4. **Context is built in.** A small dashboard and interactive map of Armenian background data (demographics, services, outcomes by region and year) sits beside the evidence, so findings read against real conditions.
5. **It is Phase 1 of a longer partnership.** Phase 2 would test whether evidence from elsewhere can guide new programs in Armenia. I will describe that as an outline and a method I am developing, not as something already proven.

## Proposed scope (about 2 to 3 months, feedback built in)

| Part | What | Roughly when | She receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1a Scoping sprint** | Fix the question, the definition of "evidence" and the inclusion rules with her. Run the search, screen and code one sector slice. Check how much the search missed. | Weeks 1 to 4 | Protocol, search log, first evidence map and gap map for one sector, recall check |
| **1b Full map** | Extend to both sectors and to grey literature (reports from international agencies, government, NGOs). Review gaps with her team. | Weeks 5 to 10 | Full evidence map and gap map, interactive version, "what looks promising" memo |
| **1c Context dashboard** | Gather background data into one linked dataset and an interactive map and dashboard. Runs alongside 1a and 1b. | Weeks 3 to 10 | Dashboard, data inventory, notes on gaps in data |
| **Transport preview** | For 2 or 3 promising interventions, outline what a Phase 2 transport assessment would need. | Weeks 9 to 10 | Short Phase 2 outline, labelled exploratory |

Two feedback checkpoints: end of week 4 (go or adjust before 1b) and mid week 8 (draft maps).

## What I will not promise

- That the map is exhaustive. I will report what the search covered and how much it likely missed.
- Predictions of what an intervention would achieve in Armenia. The preview describes what would be needed to assess that.
- Full coverage of Armenian- or Russian-language sources until I have tested the process on them.

## Questions to ask (ranked)

**Must get answered**
1. What decision will this inform (funding, program design, advocacy, a grant bid), and who reads it?
2. Which interventions is the foundation running or considering now, in education and in health?
3. What counts as evidence for her: impact evaluations only, or also qualitative studies, monitoring data and government reports?
4. What does she already hold (evaluations, monitoring data, partner reports), and can I use it?
5. Timeline, budget range and funding source. Is the map meant to support a grant application?

**Then, as time allows**
6. Geography and groups: Armenia as a whole, particular regions, displaced or other priority groups?
7. Languages the team can read and review in.
8. Preferred format: written report, interactive map, or both?
9. Who else is working on this (government ministries, UN agencies, other foundations) and should be consulted?
10. Who owns the outputs, and what can be published?
11. What does an ongoing partnership look like to her, and who would use Phase 2 results?

## Listen for

- A decision she already has in mind. It sets the sector slice for 1a.
- Existing evaluations or data. They shorten the work and shape 1c.
- Any wish for "proof" that a program works. Steer to "what is known and how strong".
- Budget signals. If small, offer 1a plus the dashboard alone, then 1b after the checkpoint.

## After the call

- Send a short written proposal within a few days, with scope, timeline, deliverables and terms.
- Ask for any documents she can share, and for introductions to her program staff.
- Decide the sector for 1a and draft the protocol (search terms, inclusion rules, categories) for her review.
