## Consulting Groups to Target:

Three different models here — treat them differently rather than one generic "portfolio + outreach" pitch.

**Project-based development consulting** — hired for a scoped piece of analytical or evaluation work, usually on a donor-funded program. Portfolio is the pitch: it has to show reproducible, evidence-rigorous work in the exact domain (climate adaptation, social protection, conflict-affected settings) these firms staff for.
- [Palladium Group](https://thepalladiumgroup.com/) — large USAID/FCDO-funded program implementer across livelihoods, governance, and economic growth. Lead with Project 02 (fused conflict/climate/displacement data) — it's the kind of applied geospatial analysis their MEL (monitoring, evaluation & learning) teams staff for.
- [IDinsight](https://www.idinsight.org/) — data-and-evidence advisory for governments and NGOs; RCT design, rapid evaluation, decision-focused analytics. Closest fit of any firm here to Project 01 (the evidence map) and the causal-ML toolkit generally — lead with those, and consider a direct application once Project 01 is live rather than waiting for them to find it.
- [Dalberg](https://dalberg.com/) — global development strategy consulting for foundations, governments, and multilaterals, with a real climate and humanitarian-response practice. Strong fit for the flagship direction (climate adaptation + conflict + displacement) — Projects 01 and 02 together make the case.
- World Bank operational consulting ([opportunities](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/corporate-procurement/business-opportunities/operational-consulting-opportunities), [context](https://www.devex.com/news/inside-the-world-bank-s-2-3-billion-consulting-contracts-in-2025-112120)) — individual short-term consultant (STC) contracts attached to specific lending operations, worth roughly $2.3B/year. No unified "apply here" — the real path is being visible to task-team leaders on climate-resilience or social-protection operations; a public toolkit (Project 04) and evidence map (Project 01) are exactly the kind of artifact that gets forwarded around a TTL's team.

**Expert networks** — a fundamentally different model: register as a paid-call expert, and clients (usually institutional investors or other consulting firms) pay per-call for a conversation, not for a delivered project. A blog doesn't source clients directly here — it increases the odds of being matched and vetted for calls in a specific topic.
- [GLG](https://glg.com/), [AlphaSights](https://www.alphasights.com/), [Guidepoint](https://www.guidepoint.com/) — register as an expert on all three once there's a public track record to point to (aim for after Project 01 or 02 ships). Keep the registered expertise tags narrow and matched to actual post topics — climate risk, agricultural technology, digital financial services/credit scoring, conflict-affected markets — rather than "development economics" generically, since these platforms route call requests by keyword match.

**Tutoring** — a different revenue stream entirely, selling teaching rather than research output.
- [Cambridge Coaching](https://cambridgecoaching.com/) — high-end academic tutoring and PhD-application coaching. The pitch here is pedagogy, not research output: the "pedagogy" post type already named in the Methods section above is what's worth pointing them to, not the project posts. Worth a direct application once 2-3 pedagogy-flavored posts exist to demonstrate teaching voice.
