# Independent research: lines of effort

*Central tracker. Created 2026-11-08 from a sweep of `main`, every local worktree, local and remote branches, and the website repo. Edit by hand until the research indexer replaces it. Each row names one line of effort; overlapping or conflicting rows are kept on purpose so they can be pruned later.*

Conventions:
- **Repo** is the project's own git repository. Separate repos are git-ignored by the monorepo and exist only in the main checkout under `projects/` (not in worktrees); their commits are pinned in [`projects/repositories.json`](projects/repositories.json). "No dedicated repo yet" means only notes or a folder exist.
- **Kind** is the reprokit project kind: `study`, `evidence`, `library`, or `-` (not a reprokit project).
- **Website** shows project pages (`content/projects/`) and blog posts (`content/blog/`) in the personal-website repo. As of this sweep the website has no research blog posts at all.
- "Not on main" means the document exists only on a branch or in a worktree and can be lost.
