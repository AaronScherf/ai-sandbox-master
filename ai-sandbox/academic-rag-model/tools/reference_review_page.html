Citation Review Queue

Each card is one DOI in one research note that the reference checker could not confirm on its own. Citations with no title pass automatically unless the same team published other work within a year; those works are listed so you can check which one the note means. Compare how the note cites it with what the registry holds, then decide. Same work carries over to the same citation in other notes; title and paper corrections stay with this note.
