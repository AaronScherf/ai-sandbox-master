## <a id="_86l97ql4eeu6"></a>Syllabus of Topics

Topics marked with a star (*) are skimmed and should be reviewed (either from textbooks or the video lectures) before the class starts. Topics with two stars (**) may not be covered within the schedule and, if omitted, will not be tested on the exam

1. [Real Analysis](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sbNE5n0BOhXHjgn0GfMof9tp7-V-AErQ/view?usp=drive_link)
	1. Sets, relations, and functions (*)
	2. Countability and cardinality (**)
	3. Open and closed sets
	4. Sequences and convergence (*)
	5. Cauchy sequences, completeness and contraction mappings
	6. Continuity of functions (*)
	7. Compactness and uniform continuity
	8. Sequences of functions and uniform convergence
2. [Linear Algebra](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LDbwQ8CBUMaLxPjuQJ2yxQzLiQq_-fln/view?usp=drive_link)
	1. Matrix algebra (*)
	2. Solutions to general linear systems (*)
	3. Vectors and vector spaces (*)
	4. Linear maps and their matrix representations
	5. Eigenvectors, eigenvalues and diagonalisation
	6. Diagonalisability and the Jordan canonical form (**)
	7. Inner product spaces and orthogonality
	8. Spectral decomposition of symmetric (unitary) matrices
	9. Quadratic forms and positive definiteness
	10. Further matrix decompositions (**)
3. [Multivariate Calculus](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-hohSslWZaDJda_SMgj0KON3LG_CG_3F/view?usp=drive_link)
	1. Derivatives (*) and differentiability of functions
	2. Mean-value theorems
	3. Implicit function theorem and inverse function theorem
	4. Taylor expansions and log-linearisation.
	5. Homogeneous functions and its characterisation
	6. Riemann integration (*)
4. [Convexity](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mjQRVWjWgsfCv3c2Zgljw7Q5xcjzoOP9/view?usp=drive_link)
	1. Convex sets (*)
	2. Convex and concave functions
	3. Quasi-convex and quasi-concave functions
5. [Static Optimization](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bM0rTJqwh38qNhEQb_FOiaq_qt9vhG5y/view?usp=drive_link) and [Correspondences](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11QuzSTTBHXRDj9xhdc9GPVAGmmcfFQfO/view?usp=drive_link)
	1. Unconstrained optimisation under differentiability (*)
	2. Constrained optimisation: Kuhn-Tucker Theorem
	3. Lagrange duality (**)
	4. Envelope theorem and comparative statics under differentiability
	5. Upper- and lower-hemicontinuity of correspondences
	6. Berge’s maximum theorem
	7. Fixed point theorems of Brouwer and Kakutani
6. Probability Theory: A Primer
	1. Probability and random variables (*)
	2. Expectation and conditional expectation
	3. Law of large numbers and central limit theorem
